Features

For students

  • One survey per class, one submission each
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktops
  • Text size and high-contrast options

For administrators

  • Manage departments, classes, faculty and students
  • Build surveys from a reusable question bank
  • Faculty-wise reports with CSV export

Privacy and anonymity

The system records that you submitted a survey (so nobody can submit twice) but stores your answers separately, with no link to your account. Administrators can see who is still pending, never who gave which rating.

Passwords are stored using salted hashing and are never visible to anyone.
